Professor Sakata spoke at THE World Academic Summit 2026

2026.10

THE World Academic Summit 2026 convened university leaders in Cape Town alongside the release of the World University Rankings. Representing the University of Tokyo, Professor Ichiro Sakata spoke in a plenary session on how universities are transforming in the age of intelligence.
